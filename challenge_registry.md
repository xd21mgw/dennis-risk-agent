## Completeness Notes

- The original 20 registry rows covered the main themes but contained merged items. This version splits ability-different items into separate challenges, especially raw inventory coverage, parsed inventory, URL/OCR/QR, account mutation chain, wave4 method family, pagination cap, strict device join, and autonomous-vs-targeted attribution.
- No challenge is marked mechanism-ready. `FULL_COVERED` only means the registry-document creation challenge itself is covered, not that a runtime mechanism exists.
- `TARGETED_ONLY` means the current evidence came mainly from user-directed follow-up, so it cannot prove full autonomous discovery.
